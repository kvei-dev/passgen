    How strong is it?

    The bit count assumes an attacker knows exactly how this generator works, so only random choices are counted. A fixed rule such as "always replace i with 1" adds nothing. Time to crack assumes 1010 guesses per second against a fast offline hash.

    • under 50 bits weak: avoid for anything important
    • 60–80 bits good for ordinary accounts
    • 80+ bits strong: master passwords, encryption keys

    Short passwords (12 characters) cannot be strong with real words in them; raise the length or the share of random characters, or use a password manager.
